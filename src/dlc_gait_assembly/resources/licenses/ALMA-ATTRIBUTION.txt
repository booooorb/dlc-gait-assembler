The ALMA-derived gait and Ladder calculations are maintained in their named
business modules. The original license is retained as ALMA-LICENSE alongside
this notice. Refactoring separates scientific calculations, persistence,
rendering, and Qt presentation. Application modifications include safe empty
results, confidence handling, marker aliases, calibration, canonical cycle
handoffs, and explicit detector/timestamp behavior. These modifications do
not imply that experimental detectors are part of the original ALMA method.

The historical spontaneous-contact comparator explicitly identifies upstream
commit 89f7be705a730fc0c0acf90817357b6449535df0 in stride_candidates.py.
Complete local source context remains under ignored references and is not a
runtime dependency or a distributed package component.

The confidence-aware runway detector, bounded joint reconstruction, native
stick-plot rendering, and treadmill input/quality checks are adapted from the
user-supplied updated ALMA source (alma-master-3, supplied November 2026).
These implementations remain GPL-3.0 ALMA derivatives. The updated runway
detector is selectable separately from the retained legacy and experimental
methods. The supplied source tree is not a runtime dependency.

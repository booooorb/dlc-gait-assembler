The ALMA-derived gait and Ladder calculations are maintained in their named
business modules. The original license is retained as ALMA-LICENSE alongside
this notice. Refactoring separates scientific calculations, persistence,
rendering, and Qt presentation. Application modifications include safe empty
results, confidence handling, marker aliases, calibration, canonical cycle
handoffs, and explicit detector/timestamp behavior. These modifications do
not imply that experimental detectors are part of the original ALMA method.
